About Sugar Concierge

We believe no one should overpay for the things they don't think about

Sugar Concierge is a New Zealand financial concierge service. Based in New Zealand, we negotiate your bills, restructure debt, and manage insurance for Kiwis across the country — finding where your money is quietly being lost and moving you to better deals, without you lifting a finger.

What we do

Your bills are our problem now.

1

We scan your finances

Connect your email and Sugar reads your bills, statements, renewals, and receipts. We build a complete picture of what you're paying and to whom.

2

We find better deals

Every rate, plan, and provider is compared against the market. We show you exactly where you're losing money and how much you could save.

3

We make the switch

Sugar handles the switching, renegotiating, and consolidating. You get better deals on everything, without lifting a finger.

Our approach

We fix the deals, not your behaviour.

Most financial tools ask you to budget harder, track every dollar, or cut things out. Sugar Concierge takes the opposite approach. We leave your lifestyle alone and fix the deals underneath it — comparing your rates against the New Zealand market to find what you should actually be paying.

Every provider, every renewal, every rate. Sugar tracks it all so you never quietly overpay again. If we don't save you money, we refund the difference. No questions asked.
