Frequently asked questions

What if Sugar doesn't deliver?

Sugar Concierge is a registered New Zealand company. We've helped hundreds of NZ customers save on bills, debt, and insurance. Read the reviews above. We're so sure we'll deliver that we'll refund the difference if we don't save you at least $4,000/yr.

Is my data safe with Sugar?

Yes. Sugar Concierge is safe and secure from the ground up. We use read-only email access, bank-level encryption, and comply with NZ's Privacy Act 2020. We never sell your data — we make money by helping you save money, that's it. You can disconnect anytime from your Google account settings.

What happens if I don't save anything?

You get your money back. Sugar Concierge is a trusted New Zealand financial service, we only win when you do. Our fee is a small share of what we actually save you. If that's less than your membership cost, we refund the difference. Hundreds of Kiwis have used Sugar and saved on bills, debt, and insurance. If we can't do the same for you, you don't pay.
