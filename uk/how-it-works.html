How it works

How Sugar Concierge Works

Sugar reviews your bills, spots where you're overpaying, and helps fix it — without leaving you to do all the legwork.

The process

From inbox to savings in four steps

No spreadsheets and no switching providers by yourself. Share your bills once, and Sugar Concierge handles the review.

1

You connect your email

It takes about a minute. You grant Sugar secure, read-only access to your inbox so we can find your financial documents like bills, statements, renewals, and receipts. We can never send, delete, or change anything.

2

We scan your finances

Our systems automatically pick out what you're paying and to whom (energy, broadband, insurance, loans, subscriptions) and build a complete picture of where your money is going each month.

3

We find where you're overpaying

We compare your current rates and plans with options available in the UK. You'll see where you could pay less and how much you could save, so you can start acting on the biggest opportunities quickly after joining.

4

We make the switch

With your approval, our concierge team handles the switching, renegotiating, and consolidating. You get better deals across the board and keep the savings. You stay in control and sign off on every change.

Safe, secure & legitimate

Is Sugar Concierge safe and legit?

Your information stays under your control. Email access is read-only, so Sugar can find financial documents such as bills, statements, and insurance renewals but cannot send, delete, or change anything in your inbox. You can also share documents by email or upload. We encrypt your data, never sell it, and never use connected-email data for advertising. You can disconnect your email at any time or revoke access from your Google Account.
