How it works

Transparent pricing, worked examples.

If we save you under £4,000

You get a refund

Our fee is 10% of whatever we save you. If that's less than what you've paid for your membership, we refund the difference. You never pay more than 10% of your savings.

Monthly billing example
Sugar saves you £2,000
Our fee (10%) £200
You paid (12 × £24.50) £294
Refund to you £94
If we save you over £4,000

10% success fee on the extra

Your membership covers the first £4,000 in savings. For anything above that, we charge a 10% success fee. You keep 90% of every extra pound.

Worked example
Sugar saves you £5,000
First £4K Covered by membership
Extra savings £1,000
Success fee (10%) £100
Your total cost £394
You keep £4,606
The Sugar Promise

We refund what we can't save you.

The membership is a 12-month term, but if you're not happy for any reason in the first two weeks, you can cancel. If we save you under £4,000 and 10% of those savings is less than the membership amount you paid, we refund the gap. Above £4,000, the membership covers the first £4,000 and a 10% success fee applies only to the extra savings.
